# Inclusion / Exclusion Criteria

## Inclusion Criteria
- IC1: Study concerns automated or LLM-assisted software test generation.
- IC2: Study evaluates generated tests, code, compilability, executability, correctness, coverage or related quality metrics.
- IC3: Study reports enough methodological detail to understand dataset/model/evaluation.
- IC4: Full text is accessible.
- IC5: English-language research publication or credible preprint relevant to the RQ.

## Exclusion Criteria
- EC1: Not related to software testing or generated tests.
- EC2: Pure opinion/blog/marketing content without empirical evidence.
- EC3: Duplicate publication of the same experiment; keep the most complete version.
- EC4: No accessible full text.
- EC5: Does not report evidence useful for the current RQ.

## Screening Rule
For every excluded full-text paper, record exactly one primary exclusion reason.
