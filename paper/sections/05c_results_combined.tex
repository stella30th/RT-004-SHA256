\subsection{Combined Results and Cross-Model Synthesis}
\label{subsec:combined_results}
For RQ1, the archived register labels 15 of the 18 eligible P0 failures as assertion-failing suites, two as runtime errors, and one as a compilation failure. These are suite-level labels: a suite with several failing assertions is counted once. Gemini CE023 is outside this cohort because its P0 task source did not match the project source.

For RQ2, ChatGPT recovered 10/11 eligible failures and Gemini 5/7. The combined focused recovery is 15/18 (83.33\%; two-sided exact 95\% Clopper--Pearson interval 58.58--96.42\%). The corresponding intervals are 58.72--99.77\% for ChatGPT and 29.04--96.33\% for Gemini. The two models have different selected failure sets, so their proportions are descriptive rather than a paired comparison.

For RQ3, the valid-cohort P0 count is 169/187 (90.37\%). Retaining those passes and adding the 15 observed P1 recoveries gives 184/187 (98.40\%), an arithmetic difference of 8.02 percentage points. P1 was not run on the P0-passing pairs, so 184/187 is a carry-forward workflow measure. The earlier 188-pair adjusted accounting treats CE023 as P0 PASS and gives 170/188 to 185/188; no successful rerun of its original P0 suite has been found, so those counts remain a sensitivity scenario. The raw-first-run scenario that also leaves CE029 and CE087 unresolved is 167/188 to 182/188 when only the 15 observed recoveries are carried forward.
